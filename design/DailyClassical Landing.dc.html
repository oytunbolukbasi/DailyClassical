One page, one job: the App Store

Same identity as the app. Paper and ink, umber as a text colour, Literata for headlines, system sans for everything else. Glass only on the date chip and the sticky capsule. No gradients, no illustration; only catalogue paintings and real app screens.
Hero. Vernet is the featured painting with the app’s date chip on its lower edge; the live chip above advances a day every few seconds and the painting crossfades (Vernet → Levitan → Friedrich). Six smaller paintings drift slowly around the headline. The Reduce Motion board shows the same stage frozen.
Phone story. Sticky phone, three steps; only the side text changes. Step 2 carries the app’s reading-focus: as the visitor scrolls, the “current” stop moves. The sticky glass capsule (icon, name, badge) appears after the hero and leaves at the closing.
Placeholders. The App Store badge is a stand-in for Apple’s official artwork (both languages). The QR square is a placeholder for the generated code. Literata is loaded from Google Fonts here only; the site self-hosts the OFL files.
