To verify: splitting Romantic and Late Romantic at 1870 is the app’s own convention (the catalogue sorts by composer: Brahms is Romantic, Bruckner, born earlier, is Late Romantic), and Rachmaninoff’s 1940 Symphonic Dances sits in Late Romantic. The dates are therefore printed with “c.” and describe the era, not every piece in it. The sentences avoid names and firsts on purpose; each is a general statement found in any standard music history.
