The Eras card is a timeline. Six strips, one painting per era, oldest on the left: Raphael, Vermeer, Fragonard, Friedrich, Monet, Bakst. Each one also appears in its own era’s plate below, so the card and the era pages agree.

How the picks were made. Each painting is the one paired with a piece by a composer of that era, and was itself painted in the era, give or take a decade. Many pairings in the catalogue reach into another century on purpose (Hokusai for La mer, Sargent for Boléro); those stay on their piece pages and out of the collages. Four painters per era, strips in date order. Composer counts are the catalogue totals; the app shows the published count.
