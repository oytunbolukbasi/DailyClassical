For: it explains the form at a glance (who is on stage, where the soloist stands), it is exact, it needs no image files and it works the same in dark mode. A museum-diagram tone. Against: a second visual language beside the paintings; violin, cello and “other” concertos look alike; eleven small drawings to design and keep consistent.

Filled dot = a player or a section. Ring = conductor or soloist. Solid shape = piano. Drawn in ink on the surface colour, in code. These five are sketches of the idea, not final drawings.
