# Support

Questions, problems or ideas: write to [hello@dailyclassical.co](mailto:hello@dailyclassical.co).

## Contact

Email [hello@dailyclassical.co](mailto:hello@dailyclassical.co) and tell us what happened, on which device and iOS version. A screenshot helps. We read every message.

## Premium on a new device

Premium follows your Apple ID, not the device. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID and open DailyClassical; Premium usually returns on its own. If it doesn't, go to **Settings › Premium › Restore purchases**.

## Cancelling the subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open **Settings › your name › Subscriptions › DailyClassical** and choose **Cancel Subscription**, at least 24 hours before the trial or the current month ends. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for. A lifetime purchase never renews.

## Refunds

Purchases are made through the App Store, so refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Your account

An account is optional; it keeps your favourites across devices. To change your password use **Settings › Account › Change password**. To delete your account and its data use **Settings › Account › Delete account**.

## Listening

DailyClassical doesn't play audio. Each guide is timed to a reference recording that opens in Spotify; times are approximate and can differ in other recordings. The daily reminder and the app's language are in **Settings › Reading**.
