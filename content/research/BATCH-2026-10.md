## Fixed choices

- **beethoven-symphony-3** reference recording: the product owner's favourite,
  `https://open.spotify.com/album/4AAP5zYQJTEFQiQacOFq2s`. Identify it from the embed page.
- Every other recording is your choice: a widely admired, complete recording on Spotify as one album.
- **vivaldi-four-seasons-spring** is *Spring* only (Concerto in E major, RV 269, three movements), form `violin-concerto`.
- **tallis-spem-in-alium** and **allegri-miserere**: form `choral`, one movement each.
- **smetana-vltava**, **ravel-bolero**: form `orchestral`, one movement. **wagner-tristan-prelude-and-liebestod**:
  `orchestral`, two movements (Prelude, Liebestod) — the purely orchestral version unless the chosen recording has the soprano; say which.
- **liszt-piano-sonata**: Sonata in B minor, one continuous movement unless the reference album splits it into tracks.
- **schubert-piano-quintet-trout**: form `chamber`. **brahms-clarinet-quintet**: `chamber`.

## Hard limits (the app relies on them)

- **At most 6 movements per piece.** A movement is one Spotify track of the reference album; stop times
  are measured from the start of that track. If the reference album splits a movement into several
  tracks, pick another recording.
- Ids exactly as in the table. `form` from the guide's list only.
- Listening-stop times cannot be checked by ear here: mark every time `≈` and list each piece in the
  research note's "Re-time" section (I add them to `retime-needed.md`).

## What you write (and only this)

For each of your pieces:

1. `content/en/pieces/<id>.md` and `content/tr/pieces/<id>.md` — full guide, format of guide §4 and §7.
   Reference examples: `rachmaninoff-piano-concerto-2`, `beethoven-piano-sonata-8`, `mozart-piano-concerto-23`.
2. `content/research/<id>.md` — verification note (same method and layout as
   `content/research/rachmaninoff-piano-concerto-2.md`): recording field check with URLs, exact track
   durations from the Spotify embed, every fact in the guide with its source, painting rights check,
   and a "Re-time" section listing every stop as an estimate.
3. `content/research/<id>.shared.md` — everything that belongs in a shared file, for me to merge:
   - the `content/paintings.yaml` entry (keys: image_url, source_url, commons_page, width, height,
     medium, license, credit_line, rights_status) for your **first-choice painting**, plus **two
     alternative paintings** (artist, title, year, collection, Commons file, pairing note) in case of a
     clash with another group;
   - new glossary rows only (terms not already in `content/en/glossary.md`), EN row and TR row in the
     exact table formats of guide §6;
   - for each new composer your group owns: the full `content/composers.yaml` entry (format at the top
     of that file: names, nationality, facts, three-paragraph bio EN and TR, portrait or `null`), with
     its sources.

**Do not edit** `content/paintings.yaml`, `content/composers.yaml`, the glossary files,
`content/schedule.yaml`, `retime-needed.md`, or anything outside your own files. Do not run `npm`
scripts, git commands or the image pipeline. Other agents write in the same folders at the same time.

## Paintings

Public domain by age, high-resolution open image (Commons or a museum open-access file), never NC.
It must share something real with the music (years, place, mood, idea). Already used (do not reuse
the same painting; another work by the same artist is fine only if it is clearly the best match):
Vernet *A Shipwreck in Stormy Seas*; Levitan *Above Eternal Peace*; Friedrich *Wanderer above the Sea
of Fog* and *The Monk by the Sea*; Repin *Barge Haulers on the Volga*; Kuindzhi *Red Sunset*; Goya
*Witches' Sabbath*; Gainsborough *The Morning Walk*; Runge, Molitor, David, Turner, Klimt, Böcklin and
Bierstadt each have one painting in `content/paintings.yaml` — check it.
