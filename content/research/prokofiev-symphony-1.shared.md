## Sergei Prokofiev (added 2026-10-24)
- Facts: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_Prokofiev · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symphony_No._1_(Prokofiev)
- Born 27 April [O.S. 15 April] 1891, Sontsovka, Bakhmut uezd, Yekaterinoslav Governorate (now Ukraine); father an agronomist managing the Sontsov estate; mother Maria a pianist. First piano piece ("Indian Gallop") at five; opera *The Giant* at nine; lessons with Glière (summers 1902–03). Entered the St Petersburg Conservatory in 1904 (aged 13), younger than most of his class, "viewed as eccentric and arrogant"; reputation as a musical rebel; Piano Concerto No. 2 scandal at Pavlovsk, 23 August 1913. 1914: won the "battle of the pianos" (Schroeder grand piano) playing his own Piano Concerto No. 1. Classical Symphony summer 1917; Violin Concerto No. 1 its exact contemporary. May 1918: left for the US with Lunacharsky's permission. *The Love for Three Oranges*, Chicago, 30 December 1921. Paris, Ballets Russes (Chout 1921, Le pas d'acier, The Prodigal Son). Married Lina (Carolina) Codina 1923, two sons (Sviatoslav, Oleg). Settled in Moscow 1936; *Peter and the Wolf* 1936; *Alexander Nevsky* 1938; *War and Peace* begun 1941. Zhdanov decree February 1948 ("formalism"); Lina arrested 20 February 1948 and charged with espionage (the bio says only that). Died 5 March 1953 (hypertensive crisis / cerebral haemorrhage), the same day as Stalin; coffin carried by hand through back streets because of the crowds.
