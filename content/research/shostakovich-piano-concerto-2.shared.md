Commons: `File:La_Leçon_de_musique,_par_Henri_Matisse.jpg` (10020 × 11846, PD-Art / PD-old-auto-expired). Pairing: a father's painting of his teenage son Pierre at the piano, with the family around and a score by Haydn on the stand; the closest match in subject. **Rights caveat:** Matisse died in November 1954, so the work is PD in the EU/Turkey only since 1 January 2025; its US status depends on first publication before 1930 (exhibited at the Salon d'Automne 1920; publication date not proven), and the Barnes Foundation's own rights code for it (4) differs from its code for clearly PD works (8). Same kind of doubt as the Malevich case; use only after checking.
