Pairing note (in the drafts): one candle burning in a dark room, the last light left; at the end of the Farewell the players snuffed out the candles on their stands one by one, until two violins were playing by the last lights.

**Alternative 1.** Joseph Wright of Derby, *An Experiment on a Bird in the Air Pump*, 1768. The National Gallery, London (NG725). Commons: `File:An_Experiment_on_a_Bird_in_an_Air_Pump_by_Joseph_Wright_of_Derby,_1768.jpg` (5639 × 4226, public domain). Pairing: painted four years before the symphony, a single candle lighting faces in the dark, the taste for drama of the Sturm und Drang years. Wright is already in `paintings.yaml` (Italian Landscape), so use only if the La Tour clashes.

**Alternative 2.** Georges de La Tour, *The Magdalen with the Night Light* (*La Madeleine à la veilleuse*), c. 1640–45. Musée du Louvre, Paris (RF 1949-11). Commons: `File:0_La_Madeleine_à_la_veilleuse_-_Georges_de_La_Tour_-_Louvre_RF_1949-11.JPG` (2536 × 3216, public domain; a gallery photo, check the edges for the frame). Pairing: the same idea, a single small flame in darkness.

## 2. Glossary

EN:

| Term | Short | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sturm und Drang | "Storm and stress": a taste for agitated, dark music | German for "storm and stress", a name borrowed from a German literary movement of the 1770s. In music it describes the agitated, often minor-key style of some of Haydn's symphonies around 1770. |
