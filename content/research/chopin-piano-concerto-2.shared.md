**Alternative 1.** Wincenty Kasprzycki (1802–1849), *Exhibition of Fine Arts in Warsaw in 1828*, 1828, National Museum in Warsaw (MP 298). Commons: `File:Wincenty Kasprzycki - Fine arts exhibition in Warsaw in 1828 - Google Art Project.jpg` (3354 × 2925, PD-Art). Pairing: Warsaw's cultured society at the great exhibition of 1828, a year before the concerto was begun (venue not checked). **Alternative 2.** After Marcin Zaleski, *View from Castle Square to Krakowskie Przedmieście*, c. 1838, National Museum in Warsaw (MP 3474). Commons: `File:Widok z Placu Zamkowego na Krakowskie Przedmieście - MP 3474.jpeg` (2500 × 1870, PD). Pairing: the street where the Chopins lived from 1827 until he left Warsaw in 1830, and where the concerto had its first run-through in the family's drawing-room. Attribution "after Zaleski" (a copy): check before use.
