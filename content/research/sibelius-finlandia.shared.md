Alternatives:

1. **Edvard Isto, *The Attack* (*Hyökkäys*), 1899.** National Museum of Finland, Helsinki (H30058). Commons: File:Hyökkäys - Edvard Isto, 1899.jpg (1136 × 1584, public domain; artist d. 1905; the museum's press image). Pairing: the Russian double eagle tears the law book from the Finnish Maiden, painted in protest against the same February Manifesto of 1899 that led to the Press Celebrations where *Finlandia* began. The best match in subject, but the scan is small: prefer it if a larger public-domain scan can be found.
2. **Eero Järnefelt, *Autumn Landscape of Lake Pielisjärvi*, 1899.** Finnish National Gallery, Ateneum. Commons: File:Eero Järnefelt - Autumn Landscape of Lake Pielisjärvi - Google Art Project.jpg (4001 × 1206, public domain). Pairing: a Karelian landscape of 1899 by Sibelius's brother-in-law. Note: already rejected once (for `sibelius-symphony-2`) as too wide (3.3:1) for the portrait hero.
