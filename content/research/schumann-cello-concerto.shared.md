### Alternatives

1. **Johann Wilhelm Schirmer, *The Path on the Edge of the Wood* (*Weg am Waldesrand*), c. 1851.** Hamburger Kunsthalle. Commons: `File:Johann Wilhelm Schirmer - Weg am Waldesrand (ca. 1851).jpg` (only 1524 × 1154; a larger image would have to come from the Kunsthalle's online collection). Schirmer (died 1863) taught landscape at the Düsseldorf Academy while Schumann was music director there. Pairing: a quiet, solitary path into a wood, from Düsseldorf in the same months.
2. **Hans Gude, *Landscape from Ringerike*, 1850.** Finnish National Gallery, Ateneum, Helsinki (A I 294). Commons: `File:Hans Gude - Landscape from Ringerige - A I 294 - Finnish National Gallery.jpg` (4000 × 2654, PD). Pairing: same year and same Düsseldorf-trained painter; a broad, calm landscape for the concerto's long-breathed song.
