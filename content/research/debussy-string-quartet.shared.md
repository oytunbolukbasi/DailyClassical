Alternatives:

1. **James McNeill Whistler, *Nocturne: Blue and Gold – Old Battersea Bridge*, c. 1872–75.** Tate Britain, London. Commons: `File:James McNeill Whistler - Nocturne en bleu et or.jpg` (1926 × 2584, PD; lower resolution, scanned from a book). Pairing: Debussy admired Whistler, and the title of his orchestral *Nocturnes* (1899) is often linked to Whistler's night pictures (check a source before using); the blue, veiled night is close to the muted Andantino. New artist for the app.
2. **Maurice Denis, *The Muses* (*Les Muses*), 1893.** Musée d'Orsay, Paris. Commons: `File:Les Muses, par Maurice Denis.jpg` (only 669 × 850; a larger photo is needed). Pairing: painted the year of the quartet by the Nabi painter who that same year designed the cover of Debussy's *La Damoiselle élue*; flat, decorative patterns repeated with variations. Denis died 1943 (PD in the EU since 2014).
