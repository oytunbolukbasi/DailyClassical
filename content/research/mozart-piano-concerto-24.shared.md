The `source_url` is the Prado's object page (found by search; it returns 403 to scripted requests). Prado record: oil on canvas, 277 × 303.4 cm, P000798, a cartoon for a tapestry for the Prince of Asturias' dining room at El Pardo; three cloaked peasants return home in the snow.

Pairing note (in the drafts): Goya designed it in 1786 for a series of the four seasons; among bright scenes of flowers, threshing and grape harvest, winter is a group of peasants struggling home through a snowstorm, as this C minor concerto was the dark work Mozart wrote beside his comic opera.

**Alternative 1.** Jacques-Louis David, *The Oath of the Horatii*, 1786 (David's reduced replica of the 1784 Louvre painting). Toledo Museum of Art (per Commons; check). Commons: `File:Jacques-Louis_David_-_Oath_of_the_Horatii_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg` (6156 × 4800, public domain; Commons dates it "1786 (original by David made in 1784)"). Pairing: the same year as the concerto, a severe, tragic drama, the grave public tone of C minor. David is already in `paintings.yaml` (two works).

**Alternative 2.** Henry Fuseli is already used for No. 20; avoid. Instead: Joseph Wright of Derby, *An Experiment on a Bird in the Air Pump*, 1768. The National Gallery, London (NG725). Commons: `File:An_Experiment_on_a_Bird_in_an_Air_Pump_by_Joseph_Wright_of_Derby,_1768.jpg` (5639 × 4226, public domain). Pairing: a dark, searching drama lit from within, faces turned between hope and dread. (Also offered as an alternative for `haydn-symphony-45`; use for at most one.)
