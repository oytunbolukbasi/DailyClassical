## Igor Stravinsky (added 2026-11-17, batch November 2026, group I)
- Facts: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igor_Stravinsky · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Firebird
- Born 17 June [O.S. 5 June] 1882, Oranienbaum (now Lomonosov), about 50 km west of St Petersburg; mother Anna, amateur singer and pianist; father Fyodor Stravinsky, bass at the Mariinsky Theatre (Wikipedia, early life). Studied law at the University of St Petersburg; met Rimsky-Korsakov in August 1902 and studied with him until his death in 1908. Engaged to his first cousin Yekaterina Nosenko in August 1905 (married 24 January 1906).
- February 1909: Diaghilev heard *Scherzo fantastique* and *Feu d'artifice* in St Petersburg; *The Firebird* (premiere 25 June 1910), *Petrushka* (1911), *The Rite of Spring* (near-riot at the Théâtre des Champs-Élysées, 29 May 1913). Switzerland (Clarens, Morges) during the First World War until 1920; Russian folk poetry (*Renard*, *Les noces*). Neoclassical period 1920–1951 (*Pulcinella* 1920, Octet 1923). French citizen June 1934.
- 1939: deaths of his daughter Lyudmila (1938), wife Yekaterina (March 1939) and mother (June 1939), as given in the Wikipedia article; moved to the US in 1939 (Charles Eliot Norton Lectures, Harvard, 1939–40); married Vera de Bosset 1940; settled in Los Angeles; US citizen 1945. Robert Craft assistant from 1948. Serial period 1954–1968 (*In Memoriam Dylan Thomas*, *Canticum Sacrum*, *Agon*, *Threni*); last major work *Requiem Canticles* (1966). Died 6 April 1971, New York (pulmonary edema); funeral at Santi Giovanni e Paolo, Venice; buried on San Michele, several metres from Diaghilev's tomb.
- Symphonies: Symphony in E-flat (1907, Op. 1), *Symphony of Psalms* (1930), Symphony in C (1940), Symphony in Three Movements (1945). Card: "Four, including the *Symphony of Psalms*" (the *Symphonies of Wind Instruments* is not counted).
- Nationality on the card: "Russian" / "Rus", as the Wikipedia lead ("a Russian composer … with French (from 1934) and American citizenship (from 1945)"); the bio gives both later citizenships.
