Alternatives:

1. **Emil Jakob Schindler, *Spring in Hacking* (*Frühling in Hacking*), 1883.** Belvedere, Vienna (Inv. 4011). Commons: `File:Emil Jakob Schindler - Frühling in Hacking - 4011 - Österreichische Galerie Belvedere.jpg` (3508 × 2336, PD-Art, artist d. 1892; photo also CC BY-SA 4.0). Pairing: blossoming trees on the edge of Vienna in early spring, by the painter whose daughter Alma married Mahler in 1902; the "Spring and no end" of the first movement.
2. **Moritz von Schwind, *The Hunter's Funeral Procession* (*Wie die Tiere den Jäger begraben*), 1850.** Woodcut for the *Münchener Bilderbogen*. Commons: `File:Schwind Begraebnis.jpg` (1468 × 541, public domain). Pairing: the animals of the forest carrying the hunter's coffin, the picture behind the third movement's funeral march. Too small for the hero image; listed for reference only.
