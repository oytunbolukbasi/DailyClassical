Alternatives:

1. **J. M. W. Turner, *Rain, Steam and Speed – The Great Western Railway*, 1844.** The National Gallery, London (NG538). Commons: File:Turner - Rain, Steam and Speed - National Gallery file.jpg (5661 × 4226, public domain). Pairing: a train charging through a storm, for a stormy symphony whose main theme came to Dvořák with an arriving train. Turner is already in paintings.yaml twice.
2. **Julius Mařák, *Šumava Virgin Forest in the Storm*, 1891–92.** National Gallery Prague (O 809). Commons: File:Julius Mařák - Šumava Virgin Forest in the Storm - O 809 - National Gallery Prague.jpg (public domain, but only 800 × 638 px: look for a larger scan first). Pairing: a dark Bohemian forest in a storm by a Czech contemporary, for Dvořák's darkest, most turbulent symphony.
