**Score.** Bar numbers from Craig Sapp's Humdrum encoding, https://github.com/craigsapp/beethoven-piano-sonatas (`kern/sonata21-1.krn`, `-2.krn`, `-3.krn`), read with a small script (bars, dynamics, text marks, key and metre changes).
