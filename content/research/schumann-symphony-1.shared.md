### Alternatives

1. **Ferdinand Georg Waldmüller, *Early Spring in the Vienna Woods* (*Vorfrühling im Wienerwald*), 1861.** Belvedere, Vienna (Inv. 1501). Commons: `File:Ferdinand Georg Waldmüller - Vorfrühling im Wienerwald - 1501 - Österreichische Galerie Belvedere.jpg` (3508 × 2856, PD; Waldmüller died 1865). Pairing: bare trees just turning green, the "world turning green" Schumann imagined for the introduction. (Waldmüller already appears once in paintings.yaml.)
2. **Ludwig Richter, *Crossing at the Schreckenstein* (*Die Überfahrt am Schreckenstein*), 1837.** Galerie Neue Meister, Dresden. Commons: `File:Dresden, Albertinum, Ludwig Richter, die Überfahrt am Schreckenstein.JPG` (4156 × 3155, photo CC BY 4.0, faithful reproduction; Richter died 1884). Pairing: a ferry full of travellers, among them a young man with a harp, crossing the Elbe below the castle ruin, by the illustrator of Schumann's *Album für die Jugend*; a Saxon Romantic scene of the years when Schumann was finding his voice.
