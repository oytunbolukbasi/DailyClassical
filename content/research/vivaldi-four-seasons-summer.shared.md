Pairing note (in the drafts): a Venetian landscape under a heavy summer sky, with lightning flickering over the town while a young man with a staff and a seated woman wait below, the uneasy calm before the storm that Vivaldi's sonnet describes.

**Alternative 1.** Pieter Bruegel the Elder, *The Harvesters*, 1565. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York (19.164). Commons: `File:Pieter_Bruegel_the_Elder-_The_Harvesters_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg` (30000 × 21851, public domain; the Met's own CC0 file `File:The_Harvesters.jpg`, 3811 × 2809, is a lighter download). Pairing: peasants cutting wheat and resting exhausted under a tree in the midday heat, "man and flock languish"; and the ripe corn that Vivaldi's hailstorm cuts down. Bruegel is already in `paintings.yaml` (*The Peasant Dance*, Beethoven 7), so use only if Giorgione is not wanted.

**Alternative 2.** Nicolas Poussin, *Summer* (*Ruth and Boaz*), from *The Four Seasons*, 1660–64. Musée du Louvre, Paris (INV 7304). Commons: search `Category:The Four Seasons (Poussin)` for the Louvre file (resolution not recorded here). Pairing: the summer of a 17th-century cycle of four seasons, harvesters in a wheat field in the heat. Gentler than the music.
