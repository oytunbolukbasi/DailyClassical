**Left out on purpose.**
- Wikipedia says Mozart adapted the Turkish episode from his ballet *Le gelosie del serraglio*, K. 135a (Milan, 1772). The current Köchel catalogue lists that ballet as a pasticcio fragment (KV Anh. A 35) whose sketches largely arrange other composers' music (e.g. Starzer). The link is too uncertain for the guide. https://kv.mozarteum.at/en/work/le-gelosie-del-serraglio-pasticcio-fragment-2557
- "Strasbourg concerto" (refers to K. 216 or K. 218, not K. 219).

**Uncertain structure in III.** The keys of the first two episodes (≈ 1:20, ≈ 2:50) are described only as "livelier" and "minor-key"; the second is believed to be in F-sharp minor but this was not checked against the score.
