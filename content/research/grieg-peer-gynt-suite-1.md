## 1. Reference recording: Karajan / Berliner Philharmoniker (DG, digital, 1982)

Why this one: Karajan's digital Peer Gynt suites are among the most widely sold and admired versions; the album gives each of the four numbers its own track, in suite order, and all are playable in Turkey. Albums of the complete incidental music (Järvi, Blomstedt) were rejected because they follow the play, include voices in "In the Hall of the Mountain King" and are not the suite. Beecham's RPO album (`5Jcohp8uFVTNxd3Ebos6kS`) mixes the two suites and the choral versions.
