Pairing note (in the drafts): music at home in the 1740s by a Berlin-born painter, a violin with harpsichord and cello beneath it, the same partnership of strings and continuo that carries Bach's concerto.

**Alternative 1.** Johann Georg Platzer, *Interior with a Concert and Card Players* (*Das Konzert*), 1730s. National Museum in Warsaw (M.Ob.1005 MNW). Commons: `File:Johann_Georg_Platzer_-_Interior_with_a_concert_and_card_players_-_M.Ob.1005_MNW_-_National_Museum_in_Warsaw.jpg` (4000 × 2851, public domain; the file shows the gilt frame edge, crop it). Pairing: a concert of strings, lute and harpsichord in a German-speaking house in the 1730s, the decade of the concerto. Busy picture with small figures.

**Alternative 2.** Thomas Gainsborough, *The Painter's Daughters chasing a Butterfly*, probably c. 1756. The National Gallery, London (NG1811). Commons: `File:File-Gainsborough_-_The_Painters_Daughters_Chasing_a_ButterflyHD.jpg` (5565 × 6000, public domain). Pairing: two sisters running hand in hand, one a step behind the other, like the two violins in the finale's canon. Gainsborough is already in `paintings.yaml` (Mozart 23), so use only if the first two clash.
