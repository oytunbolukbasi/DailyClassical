Pairing: a priest and a soldier say the service for the dead over a field of fallen men under a grey sky; a requiem, like the Largo of the symphony.
2. **Isaac Levitan, *Vladimirka*, 1892.** State Tretyakov Gallery, Moscow. Commons: File:Vladimirka.jpg (3516 × 2275, public domain; artist d. 1900). Pairing: the empty road along which convicts were marched to Siberia, under a heavy sky; suffering under the state, painted without a single figure, for a symphony that mourns quietly. Levitan is already in paintings.yaml once (*Above Eternal Peace*).
