Cover crop: wide landscape; the fallen skater is just right of centre at about 70 % of the height, so a centred crop keeps him.

Alternatives:

1. **Pieter Bruegel the Elder, *Hunters in the Snow*, 1565.** Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna (GG 1838). Commons: `File:Pieter Bruegel the Elder - Hunters in the Snow (Winter) - Google Art Project.jpg` (6819 × 4853, PD-Art). Pairing: the most famous winter picture of all, with skaters on frozen ponds below; but Bruegel is already in `paintings.yaml` (*The Peasant Dance*), and the fall on the ice is not in it.
2. **Hendrick Avercamp, *A Scene on the Ice*, c. 1625.** National Gallery of Art, Washington (1967.3.1). Commons: `File:Hendrick Avercamp - A Scene on the Ice - Google Art Project.jpg` (10718 × 5438, PD-Art). Pairing: the same subject by the same painter, a wide frozen river full of skaters; use it if the Rijksmuseum picture clashes with another group.
