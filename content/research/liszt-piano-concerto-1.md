## 0. Four movements, four tracks

The concerto is in four movements played without a break (Wikipedia; IMSLP: "4 movements"). Album choice matters: `6Ztul122w3ZQ2qOh0kPOwa` (Richter, Philips "The Two Piano Concertos / The Piano Sonata") labels its tracks "1. Allegro maestoso", "2a. Quasi adagio", "2b. Allegretto vivace – Allegro animato", "3. Allegro marziale animato", i.e. it treats the work as three movements with one split in two. The reference album `2FwoKS2mHtJZmF5QvNkyj1` labels the same four tracks I–IV, matching Wikipedia's four movements, so it is used.
