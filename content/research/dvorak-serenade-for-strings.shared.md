## 2. Glossary

EN:

| Term | Short | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Serenade | Light music in several movements, once played outdoors | Originally music performed in the evening, often outdoors, for a friend or a loved one. By the nineteenth century the name meant a relaxed orchestral or chamber work in several short movements, lighter than a symphony. |
