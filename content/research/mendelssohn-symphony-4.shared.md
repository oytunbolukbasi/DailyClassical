**Alternative 1.** Jean-Baptiste-Camille Corot (1796–1875), *The Forum Seen from the Farnese Gardens* (*Le Forum vu des jardins Farnèse*), 1826, Musée du Louvre, Paris. Commons: `File:Françoise Foliot - Corot - Le Forum vu des Jardins Farnèse.jpg` (5236 × 3078, gallery photo; check its licence tag and frame) or `File:Jean-Baptiste-Camille Corot - The Forum Seen from the Farnese Gardens.jpg` (2048 × 1154). Pairing: Rome in clear southern light, painted from life a few years before Mendelssohn's stay; the "blue sky" of the opening movement.

**Alternative 2.** J. M. W. Turner (1775–1851), *Modern Rome – Campo Vaccino*, 1839, The J. Paul Getty Museum, Los Angeles. Commons: `File:Joseph Mallord William Turner (British - Modern Rome-Campo Vaccino - Google Art Project.jpg` (4336 × 3226, PD-Art). Pairing: the Forum at sunset, a northerner's glowing vision of Italy, like Mendelssohn's. Turner is already in `paintings.yaml` (Snow Storm), so this is a fallback only.

## 2. Glossary

New term, used in IV. (Tarantella already exists.)

EN:

| Term | Short | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saltarello | A leaping Italian dance in quick triple time | A fast, jumping folk dance from central Italy, especially around Rome; its name comes from the Italian *saltare*, "to jump". |

TR:

| Key | Terim | Kısa | Tanım |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
