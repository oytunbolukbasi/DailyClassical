### Alternatives

1. **John Knox, *Landscape with Tourists at Loch Katrine*, 1815.** National Galleries of Scotland, Edinburgh (NG 2557). Commons: `File:John Knox - Landscape with Tourists at Loch Katrine - Google Art Project.jpg` (4001 × 2872, PD-Art; Knox died 1845). Pairing: early tourists in the Highlands, the romantic Scotland Mendelssohn walked through in 1829; the Classic Review links the first movement's storm to rain on Loch Katrine.
2. **Alexander Nasmyth, *A View of Edinburgh from the West*, 1822–26.** Yale Center for British Art, Paul Mellon Collection (B1976.7.134; Wikidata Q23754878). Commons: `File:Alexander Nasmyth - A View of Edinburgh from the West - Google Art Project.jpg` (7147 × 4764, PD-Art; Nasmyth died 1840). Pairing: Edinburgh as it looked a few years before Mendelssohn arrived there in July 1829 and wrote down the opening of the symphony.
