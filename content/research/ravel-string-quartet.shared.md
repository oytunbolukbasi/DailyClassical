2. **Henri-Edmond Cross, *The Cypresses at Cagnes*, 1908.** Musée d'Orsay, Paris. Commons: `File:Henri-Edmond_Cross,_1908,_Les_cyprès_à_Cagnes,_oil_on_canvas,_81_x_100_cm,_Musée_d'Orsay,_Paris.jpg` (PD, artist d. 1910; size not checked). Pairing: a southern landscape built from separate strokes of pure colour, ordered and luminous, of the same Paris generation as the quartet.
