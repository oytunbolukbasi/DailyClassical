## 2. Glossary (new term)

`content/en/glossary.md`:

```
| Trill | A rapid shake between two neighbouring notes | A rapid, even alternation between a note and the one just above it, used as decoration or to build tension. |
```

`content/tr/glossary.md`:

```
| Trill | Tril | İki komşu nota arasında hızlı titreşim | Bir notayla hemen üstündeki nota arasında hızlı ve düzenli gidip gelme; süs olarak ya da gerilimi artırmak için kullanılır. |
```

Used in: `tchaikovsky-violin-concerto` (I: the cadenza ends on a trill). EN `[[trill]]`, TR `[[trill|tril]]`. Other groups may propose the same term: keep one row. The existing `Tremolo` row stays as it is (a tremolo is a different effect).

`Attacca` is also used in this piece; its rows are in `content/research/grieg-piano-concerto.shared.md`.
