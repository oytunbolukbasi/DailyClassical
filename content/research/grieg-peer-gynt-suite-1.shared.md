1. **Theodor Kittelsen, *The Forest Troll* (*Skogtroll*), 1891–92.** Nasjonalmuseet (NG.K&H.B.06912), a drawing. Commons: `File:Theodor Kittelsen - The Forest Troll - NG.K&H.B.06912 - National Museum of Art, Architecture and Design.jpg` (4000 × 2796, PD-Art, PD-old-auto-expired). Commons: `File:Theodor Kittelsen - Far, far away Soria Moria Palace shimmered like Gold - Google Art Project.jpg` (7546 × 4955, public domain). Pairing: the Ash Lad sees the golden palace from far away; the same castle Peer promises his dying mother.
