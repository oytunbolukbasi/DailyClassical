## Fixed choices and limits

- Every recording is your choice: a widely admired, complete recording on Spotify as one album, **playable in Turkey**. This machine's network is in Turkey: in the embed page's `__NEXT_DATA__` JSON every track of the work must have `isPlayable: true`. If not, pick another recording.
- **At most 6 movements per piece**; a movement is one Spotify track of the reference album, and stop times run from the start of that track. If the album splits one movement into several tracks, pick another album. Single-movement works (overtures, tone poems, Rhapsody in Blue, Danse macabre) must be one track. Suites (Carmen, Firebird, Daphnis, Holberg, Royal Fireworks) must have at most 6 tracks on the chosen album; pick an album where that holds.
- **One exception: `beethoven-string-quartet-14` has seven movements** (played without a break). Write it with seven movements, one Spotify track each (`movement_count: 7`). The app's movement switcher gets a narrower seventh segment before this piece is scheduled; nothing else is needed from you.
- Ear-timing has been dropped for good: listening stops are estimates from the score, published analyses and the exact track lengths. Mark every time `≈`, place them as carefully as you can (bar positions × the track's average tempo), and still list them in the research note's "Re-time" section as a record of how they were derived.
- Ids exactly as above; `form` from the table.
- **Paintings:** public domain by age, high-resolution open image, never NC; a faithful photo of a PD painting is fine (EU DSM art. 14, US). It must share something real with the music. Do not reuse a painting already in `content/paintings.yaml` (114 entries; read it). Another work by an artist already there is fine only if it is clearly the best match. Give two alternatives in the shared note; another group may pick the same painting.
- Privacy: never put anyone's email or name in requests to outside services. Use `User-Agent: DailyClassical/1.0 (+https://dailyclassical.co)` where an API asks for a contact.
