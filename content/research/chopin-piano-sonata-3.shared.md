### Alternatives

1. **Rosa Bonheur, *Ploughing in the Nivernais* (*Labourage nivernais*), 1849.** Musée d'Orsay, Paris (RF 64). Commons: `File:Rosa Bonheur - Ploughing in Nevers - Google Art Project.jpg` (5414 × 2769, PD; Bonheur died 1899). Pairing: oxen ploughing in the sunlit centre of France, a painting said (en.wikipedia, "may have been inspired") to come from the opening of George Sand's *La Mare au Diable* (1846), her novel of peasant life near Nohant.
2. **Eugène Delacroix, *The Education of the Virgin*, 1852.** National Museum of Western Art, Tokyo. Commons: `File:Eugène Delacroix - The Education of the Virgin - Google Art Project.jpg` (3496 × 2897, PD). Pairing: the subject Delacroix first painted at Nohant in June 1842 for the village church, with Sand's farmer's wife and her daughter as models (Musée Delacroix); the Tokyo canvas is a later version (relationship to the 1842 canvas not confirmed).
