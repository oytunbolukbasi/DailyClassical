Alternatives:

- **A. Rudolf von Alt, *Stein an der Donau*, 1842.** Watercolour, private collection (sold at the Dorotheum, Vienna). Commons: `File:Rudolf von Alt Stein an der Donau 1842.jpg` (5000 × 2605, PD; Alt died 1905). Pairing: the little town on the Danube below Gneixendorf, where Beethoven finished this quartet in October 1826 at his brother's estate. (Watercolour, not oil; private collection.)
- **B. Carl Spitzweg, *The Bookworm* (*Der Bücherwurm*), c. 1850.** Museum Georg Schäfer, Schweinfurt. Commons: `File:Carl Spitzweg Der Bücherwurm 537.jpg` (1976 × 3543, PD; Spitzweg died 1885). Pairing: gentle Biedermeier humour, a small, private joke, like the "Must it be? It must be!" of the finale. Spitzweg already has one painting in `paintings.yaml` (*The Angler*).
