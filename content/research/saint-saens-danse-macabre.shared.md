Alternatives:

1. **Pieter Bruegel the Elder, *The Triumph of Death*, c. 1562.** Museo Nacional del Prado, Madrid (P001393). Commons: `File:The Triumph of Death by Pieter Bruegel the Elder.jpg` (2126 × 1517, PD-Art; larger Prado scans exist). Pairing: an army of skeletons sweeps away king, cardinal and peasant alike, and in the corner a skeleton plays along behind two lovers making music, Death as musician and leveller. Bruegel is already in `paintings.yaml` (*The Peasant Dance*), so use only if Notke's shape is a problem.
2. **James Ensor, *Skeletons Fighting over a Pickled Herring*, 1891.** Royal Museums of Fine Arts of Belgium, Brussels. Commons: `File:James Ensor, Skeletons Fighting Over a Pickled Herring - Musées royaux des Beaux-Arts de Belgique, Brussels.jpg` (3679 × 2207, public domain; Ensor died 1949, PD in the EU since 2020; check the US tag on the file page before use). Pairing: two grinning skulls in a grotesque squabble, the same mix of the macabre and the comic as Saint-Saëns's waltz.
