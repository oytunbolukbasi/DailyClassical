### Alternatives

1. **Thomas Cole, *The Voyage of Life: Youth*, 1842.** National Gallery of Art, Washington, Ailsa Mellon Bruce Fund (1971.16.2). Commons: `File:Thomas Cole, The Voyage of Life - Youth, 1842, NGA 52451.jpg` (4000 × 2737, CC0). Pairing: the young voyager steering towards a castle in the clouds, the hopeful "dawn of life" of the preface, before the storm.
2. **Andreas Achenbach, *Clearing Up—Coast of Sicily*, 1847.** Walters Art Museum, Baltimore (37.116). Commons: `File:Andreas Achenbach - Clearing Up—Coast of Sicily - Walters 37116.jpg` (1800 × 1298, PD; a larger CC0 image is on the Walters' own site). Pairing: a storm at sea breaking into light, painted in the years Liszt was writing the music that grew from his choruses on winds and waves.
