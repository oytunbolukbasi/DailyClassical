## 0. One movement, one track

The concerto is "one continuous movement" in three sections (Wikipedia; IMSLP: "1 movement"). Many albums split it into three tracks, which would break the rule that one movement is one track. The reference album gives it as **a single track**, so the guide has `movement_count: 1` and one movement section with the three tempo markings in its heading. The stop table therefore runs over the whole 18:55.
