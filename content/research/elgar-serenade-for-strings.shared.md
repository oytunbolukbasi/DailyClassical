**Alternative 1.** Alfred Parsons (1847–1920), *'When Nature Painted all Things Gay'*, 1887. Tate, London (N01589; Chantrey Bequest purchase 1887). Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alfred_Parsons_-_When_nature_painted_all_things_gay.jpg (1800 × 1226, PD; only modest resolution; Art UK file 1120 × 768). Pairing: an English orchard in spring blossom, painted five years before the Serenade by a painter who settled in Broadway, Worcestershire, Elgar's county.

**Alternative 2.** George Robert Lewis (1782–1871), *Hereford, Dynedor and the Malvern Hills, from the Haywood Lodge, Harvest Scene, Afternoon*, 1815. Tate, London (N02961). Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hereford,_Dynedor_and_the_Malvern_Hills_Lewis.jpg (1536 × 1090, PD). Pairing: the Malvern Hills on the horizon, the hills above the town where Elgar wrote the Serenade in 1892 (and Hereford, where he later lived). Lower resolution, and far earlier than the music.
