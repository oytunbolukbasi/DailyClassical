**Pairing.** Same title and same city as the quartet, ninety years later. Seen in the image: a dark-robed figure (Death, often read as Schiele himself) holds a young woman (often identified as Wally Neuzil, per the Commons category) who clings to him, on a rumpled white sheet over a barren, rocky ground. The tenderness of the embrace and the dread of the setting match Claudius's poem, where Death comes as a friend. The note does not claim any link between Schiele and Schubert. Klimt's *Death and Life* (Mahler 5) is the only other Viennese death image in the catalogue; a different artist and theme.

Alternatives (in `.shared.md`): Hans Baldung Grien, *Death and the Maiden* (1517, Kunstmuseum Basel); Marianne Stokes, *Death and the Maiden* (c. 1908, Musée d'Orsay).
