## 6. Re-time (every stop is ≈; derived, not heard)

Reference: Pinnock, Spotify `0TXylndVvQEFxBPJUC0ZJ7`, tracks 1–5.

Method: bar numbers from the DE Wikipedia analysis × an even tempo per section, fitted to the track lengths.
- I (464 s): slow opening bars 1–46 taken as ≈ 140 s (about 3 s per 4/4 bar at a stately Adagio), echoes from bar 13 (≈ 0:40). Remaining 324 s = Allegro bars 47–176 (130 bars) + Lentement 176–186 (11 bars, ≈ 38 s) + return of bars 47–117 (71 bars): 286 s / 201 bars ≈ 1.42 s per bar. Allegro ≈ 2:20; B minor section (bar 117) ≈ 4:00; Lentement (bar 176) ≈ 5:25; return ≈ 6:05; end ≈ 7:46 (track 7:44).
- II (95 s): 26 bars (10 + 16) played A A B B + A B = 78 bars, 1.2 s per bar: second half (bar 21 of the sequence) ≈ 0:25; return of the first part ≈ 1:03.
- III (228 s): 16 bars (8 + 8) with both halves repeated = 32 bars, 7.1 s per bar: repeat ≈ 0:57, second half ≈ 1:54.
- IV (120 s): 18 bars (8 + 10) with repeats = 36 bars ≈ 58 s, so the whole tune is played twice: second half ≈ 0:26, second time through ≈ 0:58.
- V (170 s): Menuet I and II, 16 bars each with repeats (64 bars, 2.65 s per bar, or with a da capo): Menuet II ≈ 1:10 if Menuet I comes first and has both repeats.
