Pairing note (in the drafts): the first light spreads slowly over a mountain valley, as the violin's tune rises over the still, held chord that gave the quartet its name.

**Alternative 1.** Claude Lorrain, *A Seaport at Sunrise*, 1674. Alte Pinakothek, Munich. Commons: `File:Claude_Lorrain_-_A_Seaport_at_Sunrise_-_WGA05016.jpg` (4109 × 3005, `{{PD-Art|PD-old-auto-expired}}`). Pairing: the low sun just above the sea, its light spreading over a calm harbour; the classic sunrise picture that 18th-century collectors knew.

**Alternative 2.** Jean-Joseph-Xavier Bidauld, *Sunrise over an Italian Landscape* (*Lever de soleil sur un paysage italien*), c. 1790. Collection not given on Commons (check before use). Pairing: painted within a few years of the quartet, morning light rising over a still landscape. Lower resolution than the others.
