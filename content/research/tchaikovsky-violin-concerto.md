**Cuts.** Many older recordings keep the traditional small cuts in the finale (from Auer's edition). A Gramophone review of a later Tchaikovsky/Sibelius release (seen only as a search snippet; the page returns 403 to the fetcher) says Bell and Mutter observe the traditional cuts "where Chung and Dumay open them out": https://gramophone.co.uk/review/tchaikovsky-violin-concerto-op-35 — SECONDARY. It is not certain that the review refers to the 1970 Previn recording rather than Chung's later Decca remake. The finale here is 9:29, against 10:31 for Hahn; a re-timer should check the finale against a full score.
