## Choosing the recording

The Tallis Scholars' famous 1980 recording (Gimell CDGIM 339, Merton College Chapel) is on Spotify only as *Allegri: Miserere; Palestrina: Missa Papae Marcelli; Mundy: Vox Patris Caelestis* (https://open.spotify.com/album/11EFoolEeb3cHbpugUS6dm), where the Miserere is split into 11 tracks: that breaks the one-track rule. The same 1980 performance appears as a single 12:28 track on the compilation *The Essential Tallis Scholars* (used as the alternative). The reference is therefore the ensemble's 2005 re-recording, a complete single track on a proper album.
