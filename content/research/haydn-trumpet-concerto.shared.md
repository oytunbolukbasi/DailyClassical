## 2. Glossary

EN

| Term | Short | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keyed trumpet | A trumpet with holes and keys, able to play every note | A trumpet of around 1800 with holes in its tube covered by keys, like a woodwind instrument, so that it could play a full chromatic scale. It was soon replaced by trumpets with valves. |

TR

| Key | Terim | Kısa | Tanım |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Keyed trumpet | Klapeli trompet | Delikleri ve klapeleri olan, her notayı çalabilen trompet | 1800 dolaylarında kullanılan, borusundaki delikleri tahta üflemelilerdeki gibi klapelerle kapatılan ve böylece bütün kromatik diziyi çalabilen trompet. Kısa sürede yerini pistonlu trompetlere bıraktı. |
