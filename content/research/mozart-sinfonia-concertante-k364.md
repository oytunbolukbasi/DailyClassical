**Pairing note changed.** The draft said it was "painted in the Salzburg home where Mozart wrote the Sinfonia concertante". Where the sittings took place is not documented (Leopold's letter of 20 November 1780 says Nannerl had a cold "and cannot leave the house", which suggests the sittings were elsewhere). New wording in EN and TR: painted in Salzburg in 1780–81, the year after the Sinfonia concertante; Leopold's violin and the portrait of the mother who died during the journey are what the picture shares with the music.
