Pairing: a painter's late style dissolving form into light, as Beethoven's late quartets dissolve the usual four movements into seven. Turner already has two paintings in `paintings.yaml`, so use only if A and the first choice are taken.

## 2. Glossary

EN:

| Term | Short | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sul ponticello | Bowing right next to the bridge, for a glassy, thin tone | Italian for "on the little bridge": the string player bows very close to the bridge, which brings out high overtones and gives a thin, glassy, whispering sound. |
