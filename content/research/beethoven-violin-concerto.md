## 1. Reference recording: Schneiderhan / Jochum / Berliner Philharmoniker (DG, 1962)

Chosen because it is a widely admired classic and because Schneiderhan plays **Beethoven's own cadenzas** (from the piano version, Op. 61a), adapted by him for violin, with the timpani in the first-movement cadenza. That gives the guide a concrete listening point that most recordings (with Kreisler's cadenzas) do not have.
