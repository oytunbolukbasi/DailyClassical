## 1. Reference recording: Nathan Milstein (Deutsche Grammophon, rec. 1973, rel. 1975)

Why this one: Milstein's second complete set, made for DG in 1973 and awarded a Grammy; widely regarded as a reference. One track per movement.

Two other Milstein albums on Spotify were rejected: `2FFw30wSfovVFWrhvqtvUB` ("Bach: Sonatas & Partitas", ℗ 1993 Angel Records / © 2012 EMI Classics: his earlier recording, with the dances' repeats left out, Partita 2 = 2:02 / 1:34 / 3:01 / 1:50 / 13:49) and `1nSmNay0ZHeZPC2tdWtFTT` (a 2021 "Remastered" third-party reissue, "© 2021 Alexandre Bak", with the same timings as the EMI set). Neither is the DG recording.
