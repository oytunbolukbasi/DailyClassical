Pairing note (in the drafts): a crisp autumn morning in Flanders, the sun just up and a cart setting off to market, while in the foreground a hunter with his gun and dog creeps up on a covey of partridges, as Vivaldi's hunters set out at dawn.

**Alternative 1.** Pieter Bruegel the Elder, *The Return of the Herd* (Autumn), 1565. Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna (GG 1018). Commons: `File:Pieter_Bruegel_(I)_-_The_Return_of_the_Herd_(1565).jpg` (4963 × 3593, public domain; a 40360 × 29816 file exists as `File:The_Return_of_the_Herd.jpg`). Pairing: the autumn panel of Bruegel's cycle of the months, cattle driven home under darkening skies at the end of the season. Bruegel is already in `paintings.yaml` (Beethoven 7), and *The Harvesters* is Summer's alternative 1: pick only one Bruegel.

**Alternative 2.** Francisco Goya, *The Grape Harvest* (*La vendimia*, or *Autumn*), 1786. Museo del Prado, Madrid (P000795). Commons: `File:Francisco_de_Goya_-_La_vendimia_o_El_Otoño_(1786).jpg` (2138 × 3051, public domain). Pairing: a tapestry cartoon for the season of autumn, a grape harvest with a gentleman offering grapes, the "liquor of Bacchus" of the first movement. Goya is already in `paintings.yaml` (Berlioz); smaller file.
