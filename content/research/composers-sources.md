# Composer sheet: sources (checked 2026-10-04)

Facts, bios and portraits in `content/composers.yaml`. Birth and death dates and places were cross-checked
against Wikidata (P569/P570/P19/P20) and the English Wikipedia biography of each composer. Bio claims
come from the same Wikipedia articles and Encyclopaedia Britannica's entries; nothing in the bios goes
beyond what those sources state. Portrait licences were read from the Wikimedia Commons API
(`prop=imageinfo&iiprop=url|size|extmetadata`) and the file pages.

Wikidata query used for dates/places: items Q254 (Mozart), Q255 (Beethoven), Q7312 (Schubert),
Q1151 (Berlioz), Q7294 (Brahms), Q7315 (Tchaikovsky), Q7298 (Dvořák), Q7304 (Mahler), Q80135 (Shostakovich).

## Mozart
- Facts: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfgang_Amadeus_Mozart · https://www.britannica.com/biography/Wolfgang-Amadeus-Mozart · https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q254
- "41 numbered": the traditional numbering runs to No. 41 (*Jupiter*); several numbered works are spurious and some unnumbered ones are authentic, so the card says "numbered" rather than giving a total.
- Last three symphonies written in summer 1788 (June–August); *Requiem* completed by Süssmayr.
- Portrait: Barbara Krafft (1764–1825), posthumous portrait, 1819, oil on canvas, Gesellschaft der Musikfreunde, Vienna.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Barbara_Krafft_-_Portr%C3%A4t_Wolfgang_Amadeus_Mozart_(1819).jpg — 1447 × 1784, Public domain (PD-Art, life + 100).

## Beethoven
- Facts: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig_van_Beethoven · https://www.britannica.com/biography/Ludwig-van-Beethoven · https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q255
- Born: only the baptism (17 December 1770) is documented; Wikidata gives 16 December as the probable birth date. The card says "December 1770, Bonn" to stay exact.
- Heiligenstadt Testament (October 1802); hearing loss from c. 1798.
- Portrait: Joseph Karl Stieler (1781–1858), *Beethoven with the Manuscript of the Missa solemnis*, 1820, oil on canvas, Beethoven-Haus Bonn (B 2389).
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Joseph_Karl_Stieler%27s_Beethoven_mit_dem_Manuskript_der_Missa_solemnis.jpg — 4330 × 5389, Public domain (PD-Art).

## Schubert
- Facts: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Schubert · https://www.britannica.com/biography/Franz-Schubert · https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q7312
- Wikidata birthplace: Himmelpfortgrund (then a suburb, now part of Vienna's 9th district); the card says Vienna.
- "Seven complete symphonies" (Wikipedia), plus the *Unfinished*. *Erlkönig* 1815 (aged 18). *Unfinished* first performed 1865, *Great* C major 1839.
- Portrait: Wilhelm August Rieder (1796–1880), oil, 1875, after his own 1825 watercolour; Wien Museum, Vienna (per the Commons file page).
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Franz_Schubert_by_Wilhelm_August_Rieder_1875_larger_version.png — 583 × 710, Public domain (PD-Art). Note: the Commons page flags missing source information for the scan; the painting itself is unquestionably PD. Lower resolution than the others; a better scan (or the 1825 watercolour, ÖNB, File:Franz_Schubert,_watercolor_portrait_by_Wilhelm_August_Rieder,_1825.jpg, 798 × 710) could replace it.

## Berlioz
- Facts: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hector_Berlioz · https://www.britannica.com/biography/Hector-Berlioz · https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1151
- Four symphonies: *Symphonie fantastique*, *Harold en Italie*, *Roméo et Juliette*, *Grande symphonie funèbre et triomphale*. *Les Troyens* never staged complete in his lifetime (acts 3–5 only, 1863). *Grand traité d'instrumentation* (1844).
- Portrait: Gustave Courbet (1819–1877), *Portrait of Hector Berlioz*, 1850, oil on canvas, Musée d'Orsay, Paris (RF 2320).
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gustave_Courbet_-_Portrait_of_Hector_Berlioz_-_WGA05492.jpg — 1000 × 1238, Public domain (PD-Art).

## Brahms
- Facts: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Brahms · https://www.britannica.com/biography/Johannes-Brahms · https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q7294
- The bio deliberately avoids the disputed story that the teenage Brahms played in harbour brothels; it says only that he earned money at the piano.
- Schumann's article "Neue Bahnen" (October 1853). First Symphony premiered 1876 (Brahms 43). Clara Schumann died May 1896, Brahms April 1897.
- Portrait: photograph by C. Brasch, Berlin, 1889. No painted portrait of comparable quality was found on Commons.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:JohannesBrahms.jpg — 1462 × 1914, Public domain (published before 1931; PD-old-70). A documented NYPL copy exists at lower resolution: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Johannes_Brahms_portrait.jpg (NYPL digital ID 1158325). No collection is shown in the caption.

## Tchaikovsky
- Facts: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyotr_Ilyich_Tchaikovsky · https://www.britannica.com/biography/Pyotr-Ilyich-Tchaikovsky · https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q7315
- Dates are New Style (born 25 April O.S., died 25 October O.S.). Von Meck allowance 1877–1890. *Pathétique* premiere 28 October 1893, death 6 November. Carnegie Hall opening, May 1891.
- Portrait: Nikolai Kuznetsov (1850–1929), *Portrait of Tchaikovsky*, 1893, oil on canvas, State Tretyakov Gallery, Moscow (Ж-2595). This is the portrait the design uses.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Portr%C3%A4t_des_Komponisten_Pjotr_I._Tschaikowski_(1840-1893).jpg — 1920 × 2466, Public domain (life + 95; published 1906 in Modest Tchaikovsky's *Life & Letters*, so PD in the US). The Commons metadata date "1906" is that publication; the painting is 1893.

## Dvořák
- Facts: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton%C3%ADn_Dvo%C5%99%C3%A1k · https://www.britannica.com/biography/Antonin-Dvorak · https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q7298
- Wikidata death place: Nové Město (New Town), Prague. Provisional Theatre orchestra (Smetana conducted from 1866). Austrian State Stipendium (Brahms on the jury); *Slavonic Dances* published by Simrock 1878. Nine visits to England. New York 1892–1895; Spillville summer 1893; director of the Prague Conservatory 1901; *Rusalka* 1901.
- Portrait: photograph, unknown photographer, 1882, scan from Gallica (Bibliothèque nationale de France, btv1b8417521d).
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dvorak.jpg — 2100 × 2600, Public domain (anonymous 1882 photograph). Rejected: Emanuel Nádherný's 1893 watercolour (file is CC BY-SA 4.0 and Nádherný died 1945, not PD everywhere); Raimund Wolf's 1890 portrait (auction-site scan, 605 × 807).

## Mahler
- Facts: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustav_Mahler · https://www.britannica.com/biography/Gustav-Mahler · https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q7304
- Fourteen children, many died in infancy. Vienna Conservatory 1875. Conversion February 1897; Vienna Court Opera 1897–1907. "The symphony must be like the world. It must embrace everything" is reported by Sibelius from their 1907 meeting, hence "is said to have told Sibelius". Met Opera and New York Philharmonic 1908–1911. Nationality: born in Bohemia (Austrian Empire), worked as an Austrian; shown as "Austrian".
- Portrait: Akseli Gallen-Kallela (1865–1931), *Portrait of Gustav Mahler*, 1907, Gösta Serlachius Fine Arts Foundation (Serlachius Museums), Mänttä.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Akseli_Gallen-Kallela_-_Portrait_of_Gustav_Mahler_-_G%C3%B6sta_Serlachius_Fine_Arts_Foundation.jpg — 1312 × 2000, Public domain (PD-Art, life + 80).

## Shostakovich
- Facts: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitri_Shostakovich · https://www.britannica.com/biography/Dmitri-Shostakovich · https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q80135
- Cinema pianist after his father's death (1922). First Symphony premiered 1926 (aged 19); Bruno Walter (Berlin) and Stokowski (Philadelphia) soon took it up. *Pravda* editorial "Muddle Instead of Music", January 1936. Fifth Symphony 1937. Zhdanov decree 1948. Leningrad performance of the Seventh, 9 August 1942. Fifteen symphonies, fifteen string quartets.
