1. **Léon Bakst, *Landscape with Shepherds*, set design for the ballet *Daphnis et Chloé*, [1911].** The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York, Gift of Sir Joseph Duveen, 1922 (22.226.2). Watercolour, 33.3 × 50.2 cm. Commons: `File:Landscape with Shepherds, Set Design for the Ballet 'Daphnis and Chloé', performed at the Théâtre du Châtelet in Paris, 1912 MET DP804799.jpg` (3876 × 2512, uploaded as CC0 from the Met). Pairing: a shepherdess and a goatherd among their flock under a deep blue sky, Bakst's vision of the lovers' world. Caveat: the Met's collection API now returns `isPublicDomain: false` for this object (no image served), although Bakst died in 1924 and the work is PD by age; the photo of a 2-D PD work carries no new right (EU DSM art. 14, US).
2. **François Boucher, *Pan and Syrinx*, 1759.** The National Gallery, London. Commons: `File:François Boucher - Pan and Syrinx, 1759.jpg` (5405 × 4186, PD). Pairing: Pan reaching for the nymph Syrinx as she hides among the reeds, the very story the lovers act out in the Pantomime.
