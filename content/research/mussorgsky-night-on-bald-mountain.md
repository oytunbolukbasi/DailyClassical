**Version.** Rimsky-Korsakov's "fantasy for orchestra" (1886), as the brief asks. The reference album's track is titled simply "A Night on Bald Mountain"; Reiner's *Festival* LP and every standard concert performance of the period use Rimsky's version, and the 10:14 duration and the bell/dawn ending fit it (Mussorgsky's 1867 original has no dawn ending). Not the 1867 original or Stokowski's *Fantasia* arrangement. Rejected: Abbado / Berlin Philharmonic on 2JclVmyGd0mGSJEgCMVeVs (track 16, "St. John's Night on the Bare Mountain (Version for Choir & Orchestra)", the 1880 Sorochyntsi version, not Rimsky's).

`year: 1886` is the year of Rimsky-Korsakov's version, the one played; the guide's big picture gives 1867 and 1880 for Mussorgsky's own versions.
