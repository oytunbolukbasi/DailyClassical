**Why this painting.** Painted in 1918, the year before the concerto, by a soldier-artist; a pale sun rising over the destroyed wood. Elgar's concerto, written in 1918–19 "in the aftermath of the First World War" and "contemplative and elegiac" (Wikipedia), mourns in a private voice; the painting states openly what the music implies. No direct link between Nash and Elgar is claimed.
