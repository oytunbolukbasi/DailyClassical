## Ralph Vaughan Williams (added 2026-10-24)
- Facts: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Vaughan_Williams · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lark_Ascending_(Vaughan_Williams)
- Born 12 October 1872, Down Ampney, Gloucestershire, son of the vicar Arthur Vaughan Williams; mother Margaret née Wedgwood, great-granddaughter of Josiah Wedgwood and niece of Charles Darwin. Father died February 1875; family moved to Leith Hill Place, Surrey. Piano lessons from his aunt Sophy Wedgwood at five; disliked the piano, preferred the violin. RCM from 1890 (Parry), Cambridge (BMus 1894, BA 1895), back to the RCM. "Musically a late developer, not finding his true voice until his late thirties". Folk-song collecting from 1903–04; influence of modes and of Tudor/Stuart music. Three months with Ravel in Paris, winter 1907–08; Ravel: "my only pupil who does not write my music". 1910: Tallis Fantasia (Three Choirs Festival, Gloucester Cathedral, September) and A Sea Symphony (Leeds, October). Volunteered August 1914, approaching 42; RAMC stretcher bearer in France and Greece; 1917 commissioned in the Royal Artillery, action in France from March 1918; guns damaged his hearing. Taught at the RCM from 1919 for twenty years. Nine symphonies; the Ninth premiered April 1958. Married Adeline 1897; Adeline died 1951; married Ursula Wood February 1953. Died 26 August 1958, Hanover Terrace, London, aged 85; ashes interred near Purcell and Stanford in Westminster Abbey.
