Exposition repeat: Pollini's I (7:16) against Rubinstein's (5:36, no repeat) shows the repeat is taken. Wikipedia: the London and Paris first editions start the repeat at the Grave, the Leipzig edition at the Doppio movimento; "most commercial recordings exclude the Grave from the repeat". The guide's times assume Pollini goes back to the Doppio movimento; confirm by ear.

"Pollini was 42": born 5 January 1942, recorded September 1984. 1960 Chopin Competition at 18; Rubinstein, head of the jury: "that boy can play the piano better than any of us" (paraphrased in the guide). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurizio_Pollini. OK.
