**Pairing facts.** Central panel: Christ in judgment on a rainbow; below, the Archangel Michael in armour weighs the souls (museum site: Michael "weighs the souls", "dressed in a golden Milanese armour"); four angels blow the trumpets of the Apocalypse, three in the centre and a fourth moved to the right wing (pl.wikipedia: "Trzej kolejni aniołowie… oraz czwarty (przeniesiony na prawy panel) dmą w trąby Apokalipsy"); left wing, the saved led by St Peter to the gate of heaven; right wing, the damned falling into hell. The triptych was commissioned by Angelo Tani for a chapel of St Michael at the Badia Fiesolana and was captured at sea by the Danzig privateer Paul Beneke (Wikipedia). Requiem texts it illustrates: Dies irae, Tuba mirum, Confutatis ("flammis acribus addictis"), and the Offertory's "signifer sanctus Michael repraesentet eas in lucem sanctam". Pairing note uses only the trumpets, Michael weighing souls, and the Offertory text.
