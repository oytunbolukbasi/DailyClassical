## 1. Reference recording: Kubelík / Bavarian Radio Symphony Orchestra (DG, 1967)

Why this one: Kubelík's first recording of the symphony, from his DG Mahler cycle, is a long-standing favourite (DG reissued it in The Originals). Exposition repeat in I: not stated anywhere I could check.
